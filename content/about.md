Hi, I'm **Kaitao Song**, currently serving as a Senior Research Scientist at Apple since May 2025. Previously, I was a Senior Researcher at Microsoft Research Asia since July 2021, focusing on AI Agents, Large Language Models, and related topics. Before that, I received my Bachelor and PhD degree at Nanjing University of Science and Technology in 2015 and 2021. 
My research interests include Foundation Models, Large Language Models, AI Agents, and NLP more broadly. 

Feel free to reach out via [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kaitao-song-720147b7/) or [X](https://x.com/SongKaitao).
